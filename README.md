# Retail_Sathi_demo
It is an interface demo of a AI agentic retail shop.
RetailSAATHI is a modern, AI-inspired retail web application built with React.js and Tailwind CSS.
It helps both customers and store managers efficiently browse, manage, and analyze products.
From intelligent product recommendations to cart management and analytics — SmartRetailAI combines simplicity, speed, and smart design.


Key Features 
Customer Side ----
|| Home Page: Displays trending and available retail products with search & filter options.
|| Smart Search: Quickly find products by name or brand.
|| Product Detail Page: Shows product image, price, description, and live stock.
|| Cart Management: Prompts for quantity before adding to cart
Automatically calculates total price (price × quantity)
Displays full product list and grand total in the cart
Allows removing items anytime
|| Wishlist: Save products for later purchase (duplicates prevented).
|| AI Recommendation (Concept): Recommends similar or trending items based on user interest.

 
 Manager Side---
|| Dashboard: Overview of retail analytics and product statistics.
|| Inventory Management: Add, edit, or delete products.
|| Analytics: Visual reports and performance insights (future integration).
|| Promotions: Manage offers and discounts.
|| Agent Logs: View agent activities and operational logs.

It is just a demo UI ... more featuures and complex services are coming soon....
